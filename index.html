Our Approach

A holistic approach to lasting career success

We look beyond the resume. Our coaches work with you on the practical and the personal — because real success on the job depends on more than just landing the interview. From eligible students preparing for life after high school to job seekers and professionals with advanced degrees, we help people build the skills, confidence, and independence to move forward.

  • Job identification & application support
  • Anxiety management & confidence building
  • Self-advocacy skills training
  • Independent living skills
  • Travel training to & from work
  • Ongoing on-the-job coaching
"We meet clients where they are — at community sites like local libraries and One-Stop Career Centers, and later, the coach is available for onsite coaching at the workplace once they're placed or offsite, if preferred."

What's next for your student?

Helping eligible students prepare for life after high school.

Pre-Employment Transition Services

Internships / Job Exploration

Discover careers that match your interests, strengths, and abilities. Students explore career pathways, take vocational assessments, learn about labor market information, identify in-demand occupations, and develop realistic career goals through individualized guidance and hands-on activities.

Counseling — Postsecondary Opportunities

Explore education and training options after high school. Students receive guidance on college, vocational and technical schools, apprenticeships, certification programs, financial aid, admissions, disability support services, and other pathways that lead to meaningful careers.

Workplace Readiness Training

Develop the essential skills needed for success in the workplace. Training focuses on communication, teamwork, problem solving, professionalism, time management, workplace etiquette, financial literacy, transportation, technology, and other skills employers value.

Students learn how to build a resume, write a cover letter, prepare references, and develop other valuable pre-vocational skills.

Instruction in Self-Advocacy

Build confidence and independence by learning how to communicate your needs, understand your rights and responsibilities, make informed decisions, request accommodations, set personal goals, and advocate for yourself in school, work, and the community.

For more information, to discuss eligibility, or to enroll, contact us.
